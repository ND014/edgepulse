Retail bookmakers (DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM) lag behind global sharp market-makers. When their odds are priced too high, they offer positive Expected Value (+EV) where you hold the statistical advantage over the house.

Never guess your stake size. Enter your bankroll in the top bar. Our Kelly Criterion calculator computes the exact dollar amount to bet to grow your bankroll while avoiding drawdown.
